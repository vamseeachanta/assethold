Investment Highlights

In 2017, the VA established operations at this location, delivering a full build-out to conform with rigorous federal government specifications.
The facility operates as a Community-Based Outpatient Clinic (CBOC), providing primary care, mental health services, and a range of specialty outpatient services, supported by seven providers across approximately 9 specialties.
Serves a regional veteran population across western Kentucky and southern Illinois.
Functions as a key rural healthcare hub with a multi-state catchment spanning KY, IL, MO, and parts of TN.
VA leases have demonstrated historically high renewal rates, generally ranging between 80% and 90%.
