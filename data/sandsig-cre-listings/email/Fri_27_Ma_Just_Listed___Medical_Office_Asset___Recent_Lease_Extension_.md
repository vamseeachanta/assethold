Investment Highlights

The location has operated as an optometry practice serving the Medina community since 1990, originally as Medina Vision Centre.
Clarkson Eyecare, founded in 1979, is a leading provider of medical optometry services with 230+ locations across the United States.
A recent lease extension demonstrates the tenant's continued commitment to this premier medical office location, further reinforcing the strength of the site and its long-standing operating history.
The property is located approximately 1.4-miles from Cleveland Clinic Medina Hospital, a 148-bed acute care facility and part of the nationally recognized Cleveland Clinic health system.
