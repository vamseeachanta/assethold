Investment Highlights

Clarkson Eyecare, founded in 1979, is a leading provider of medical optometry services with 230+ locations across the United States. The company is part of EyeCare Partners, one of the largest vertically integrated eye-care platforms in the country with 700+ locations.
Located approximately 5 miles from Summa Health Barberton Campus, a 64-bed acute care hospital providing emergency services, surgical care, imaging, and specialty medical treatment to the greater Akron area.
A recent lease extension demonstrates Clarkson Eyecare's continued commitment to this premier medical office location.
The property sits on a 2.85-acre parcel (124,146 SF) and includes a 3,827-SF single-tenant building, providing a significant amount of excess land relative to the current building footprint.
