Investment Highlights

Positioned along one of Memphis' well-traveled commercial corridors, 6645 E Shelby Drive, Memphis, TN 38141, presents a stabilized retail investment opportunity in the established Hickory Hill submarket.
The property consists of a 6,828 SF retail building situated on a 0.89-acre (53,542 SF) site. Currently occupied by Beauty Trendz, the asset provides steady in-place tenancy within a high-traffic retail environment.
Built in 1998 and zoned CA (Commercial-Agricultural), the property combines durable construction with flexible commercial zoning, supporting long-term occupancy and potential future redevelopment options.
Located in the established Hickory Hill neighborhood in southeast Memphis, an area with a strong mix of residential, retail, and light industrial uses that supports steady traffic and a solid consumer base.
Positioned along E Shelby Drive, a key arterial roadway that
provides excellent connectivity to surrounding neighborhoods and major routes across Memphis.
