Investment Highlights

This opportunity encompasses the future development of two mixed use apartment buildings located within one mile of each other in the heart of downtown Battle Ground, Washington.
The properties offer excellent regional connectivity, situated just 7.4 miles from Interstate 5 (I-5), 10 miles from Interstate 205 (I-205), and 23 miles from Interstate 405 (I-405).
The sites provide convenient access to major destinations, including Portland International Airport (19 miles), the City of Vancouver, WA (18 miles), the City of Portland, OR (27 miles), the City of Seattle, WA (162 miles), and the Washington-Oregon state line (19 miles).
The mixed-use zoning designation allows for a flexible range of residential, retail, and commercial
development possibilities.
