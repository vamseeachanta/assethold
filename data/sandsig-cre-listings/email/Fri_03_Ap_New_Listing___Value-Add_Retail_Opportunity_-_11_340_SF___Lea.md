Investment Highlights

10 S Morgan Ave is a multi-tenant commercial property totaling approximately 11,340 square feet.
The property is divided into seven units, with one currently vacant, resulting in approximately 86% occupancy. The available suite spans roughly 1,200 square feet, presenting immediate value-add potential through lease-up.
Situated on a 0.75-acre parcel, the asset includes approximately 30 surface parking spaces and is zoned commercial (NEC). The layout allows for flexible tenant use and convenient access for both customers and occupants.
Located in Andrews, South Carolina, within Georgetown County, the property benefits from connectivity to key regional markets, including Georgetown, Myrtle Beach, and Charleston via nearby thoroughfares.
