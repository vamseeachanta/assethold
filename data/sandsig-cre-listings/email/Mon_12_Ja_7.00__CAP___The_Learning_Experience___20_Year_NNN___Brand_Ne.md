Investment Highlights

This property is a new 2025 build-to-suit and is 100% leased to The Learning Experience on a Triple Net (NNN) Lease with 20 years remaining, featuring 10% rental escalations every five years, and with the only landlord responsibility being the structure.

The property is ideally positioned on a 1.12-acre lot between several large suburban neighborhoods and many key employers in the area. Denton County is one of the more affluent counties in the United States, with an annual household income of over $156,363.
