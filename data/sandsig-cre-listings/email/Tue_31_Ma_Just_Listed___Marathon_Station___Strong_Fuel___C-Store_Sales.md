Investment Highlights

Long-Term Brand Stability: The property is secured by a 12-year Marathon-branded Retail Petroleum Supply Agreement (RPSA) executed in August 2025.
Strategic Interstate & Route 66 Location: Positioned along I-40 and historic Route 66, one of the most heavily traveled east-west corridors in the U.S., the Santa Rosa site benefits from consistent transient traffic and serves as a primary fuel stop in Guadalupe County, a market with limited direct competition.
Proven and Stable Fuel Volumes: The site shows strong and consistent performance, with current fuel sales of approximately 70,000 gallons per month and trailing 2024 volumes around 75,000 gallons per month.
Diversified Revenue Stream: In addition to fuel income, the convenience store generates approximately $30,000 per month in inside sales.
