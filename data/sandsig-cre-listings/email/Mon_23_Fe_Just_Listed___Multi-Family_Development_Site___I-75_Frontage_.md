Investment Highlights

Prime development site suitable for traditional multi-family, student housing, or build-to-rent concepts within one of Valdosta's most active growth corridors.
The property features frontage along I-75 with approximately 60,000 vehicles per day, offering exceptional visibility and accessibility that is rarely available for multi-family sites.
Seller has previously approved plans for a student housing development, providing a meaningful head start on design, entitlement, and overall development timeline.
Located near Valdosta State University and Moody Air Force Base, two of the area's largest and most stable housing demand drivers.
The site is within the vicinity of established apartment communities, retail, medical, and daily-needs amenities.
